If you're reading this, you're me, later. Hi.

I made this corner of the site because some thoughts don't deserve a blog post and aren't quite a rant either. They're just things I want to be able to find again — a decision I'm proud of, an idea I don't want to lose, a feeling worth a timestamp.

The countdown on the home page isn't a gimmick. Time really is running out, a little, every day. So this is the deal:

> Write the thing down. Future you can't remember what present you didn't bother to save.

That's it. No schedule, no audience to perform for. Just receipts for my own head.
